# DSH McDonald's MCP Promo

## Style Prompt

Light, precise product-demo graphics with the warmth of a food brand and the discipline of developer tooling. Use a clear editorial grid, real plugin interface details, restrained motion, and red/yellow/black brand cues. This is an independent DSH plugin demonstration, not an official McDonald's product announcement.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#FFF9E9`
- Ink: `#171717`
- McDonald's yellow: `#FFC72C`
- McDonald's red: `#DA291C`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted text: `#68635A`

## Typography

- Chinese headlines and UI: Microsoft YaHei UI, bold and regular weights
- Commands and tool names: Consolas, bold and regular weights

## Motion

Use clean horizontal wipes, short block reveals, and crisp UI state changes. Keep transitions under 0.7 seconds and hold important copy for comfortable reading.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use the McDonald's arches or imply official endorsement.
- Do not show an actual token, fabricated live price, or completed order.
- Do not imply that `mcd_plan_order` searches the full menu or guarantees a global minimum.
